How Minecraft Began

Minecraft is a sandbox video game developed and published by Mojang Studios. Initially created by Markus Persson, known as "Notch," the game was first released to the public on May 17, 2009, as a public alpha for PC. Minecraft went on to officially release on November 18, 2011, after two years of development.

The game was inspired by a variety of other games, including Dwarf Fortress, Infiniminer, and RollerCoaster Tycoon. Its core idea was to provide a sandbox environment where players could mine resources, craft items, and build structures in a procedurally generated world made entirely of blocks.

Minecraft became a massive success thanks to its unique gameplay, constant updates, and strong community. Over time, the game expanded to multiple platforms, including consoles, mobile devices, and VR. Today, it remains one of the best-selling video games of all time, with millions of players across the globe.
